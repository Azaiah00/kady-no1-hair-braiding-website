FAQ

Good to know before you book.

Still have a question? Call (804) 261-0313 or send us a message.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome. Booking on Booksy is the best way to guarantee your time, especially for longer styles like small knotless.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes. We are open Sundays by appointment, 8am to 6pm. Book on Booksy or call (804) 261-0313.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday 6am to 9pm, and Sunday 8am to 6pm by appointment.

How much do knotless braids cost?

On our Booksy menu, knotless braids start at $160 for large, bra-strap length. Medium is $220, small-medium $260 and small $360, with braiding hair provided.

Is braiding hair included?

For most braids, twists and soft locs on our menu, yes, we provide the braiding hair. For boho styles you bring the curly hair, and for sew-ins and crochet you bring your own hair.

How should I prepare my hair?

We recommend arriving with your hair washed, blown out and detangled. If you would rather we handle it, add Detangle & Blow Dry ($30) when you book.

Will my price change at the chair?

Your price is set by the style, size and length you choose on Booksy, and add-ons are listed there too. Your braider confirms the total with you before starting.

Do you braid kids and men?

Yes. Kids' cornrows and kids' box braids are on the menu, and men's cornrows start at $40.

Do you start and maintain locs?

Yes, we start and maintain locs, and we also install soft locs and faux locs.

Where is the salon?

We are at 7601 Brook Rd, Ste F, Richmond, VA 23227, on the Brook Road corridor in North Richmond near the Henrico line.
