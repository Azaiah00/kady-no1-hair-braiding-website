Prep guide

How to arrive ready.

  1. Wash and condition

    We recommend coming in with freshly washed hair, free of heavy product build-up.

  2. Blow out and detangle

    Stretched, detangled hair gives the cleanest parts. Short on time? Add Detangle & Blow Dry ($30) or Shampoo & Blow Dry ($30) to your booking.

  3. Bring curly hair for boho

    For boho braids you bring the curly hair and we provide the braiding hair. For medium boho, you can also buy curly hair here.

  4. Plan for the time

    Booksy lists how long each style takes, from 35 minutes to a full day for micro styles. Bring a charger; the salon has Wi-Fi.

Visit FAQ

Before you head over.

Is there parking?

Yes. On-site parking is available at the salon at 7601 Brook Rd.

Is the salon wheelchair accessible?

Our Booksy listing notes the salon is accessible for people with disabilities. Call ahead if you need anything to be ready when you arrive.

What if I am running late?

Call us right away at (804) 261-0313 so we can adjust your appointment. Longer styles are timed to the minute, so a quick call helps us keep your spot.

Do I need a deposit?

Any booking requirement, including a deposit if one applies to your style, is shown on Booksy before you confirm.

Can I bring my children?

Our Booksy listing notes the salon is child-friendly. Kids can also book their own cornrows or box braids.
